# Method evolution

For timing or throughput lessons, use [Progress and performance](performance.md).
Its references are part of this same evolvable plugin. Improve the measurement
or decision method from current-run evidence; keep workload-specific settings
conditional and check later outcomes rather than claiming benefit at acceptance.

Use closed Attempt evidence, accepted reports and original errors within your
scope to identify a concrete recurring or costly methodological weakness. Check
the report's premise against the source. API unavailability alone is not a skill
defect. Do not copy full transcripts, transient capacity or live log ages into
stable guidance. A focused change or no change is a valid outcome.

Only SELF_EVOLVE for an expert and GRAPH_EVOLVE for the Grounding manager grant
/plugin-update. Use the existing self-evolve skill and Host Git candidate path.
You may improve this research-workflow entry and its references in your own
plugin, including stage routing, while preserving wired entrypoints, ownership,
byte limits and task permissions. Avoid duplicating specialist instructions.

The Host validates structure and ownership, not the quality of a new heuristic.
Accepted revisions become selectable for later requests/cycles under existing
pin/revert/unpin rules; already-bound requests keep their version. Never change
accepted evaluators, thresholds, framework validators or another owner's plugin.

Share evidence-backed lessons through existing reports. Private revisions are
not automatically imported into new runs or other users' plugins. Public seed
improvements require ordinary source review and cross-task validation. Later
evolution cannot retroactively shorten initial Grounding.
