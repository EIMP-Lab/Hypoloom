# Analysis, decisions and reports

Use accepted outcomes and original evidence relevant to the current question.
Check metric direction, splits, aggregation, configuration and comparison scope
before concluding that one result improves another. Identify missing evidence
explicitly. A passing local test, technical closure or model narrative does not
establish an accepted scientific result or complete shared-goal success.

Explain what the evidence supports, what it contradicts and what remains open.
Recommend the next discriminating action within this node's authority. Leader
experience informs peers; it does not grant control over their work. Observer
and judge decisions retain their existing source and permission constraints.

Use the existing report/interaction skill and required schema. Preserve useful
lessons and exact source references in existing accepted reports or memory paths;
do not create another evidence database, scorecard or copy of full transcripts.
